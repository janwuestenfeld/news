Daily readings at the close of each day (24:00 New York time), 1 Aug 2025 to 5 Nov 2026. Every 0–100 reading compares the day with comparable days: the same kind of day (weekday or weekend) in the previous 26 weeks; 50 is a typical day.

How much more widely than usual the day's top topic is covered: the topic furthest above its usual number of sources (0–100 against comparable days)

News Sentiment

Balance of positive and negative news across topics (0–100; low = more negative than usual)
