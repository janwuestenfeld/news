News Pulse reads the headlines of 111 English news sources on Bluesky and Telegram, minute by minute, across ten topics. For each topic and for the day as a whole it shows:

  • News Heat: how widely the news is covered
  • Breadth: how serious it is
  • News Sentiment: whether it is positive or negative
  • News Velocity: how fast it arrives

Each reading compares today, up to the current time, with the same hours on comparable days. When coverage and seriousness are both high, the Confluence tracker marks a shock day, and calls it negative or positive when the news also leans clearly one way.
